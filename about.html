About

MIAD works at the intersection of AI strategy and creative direction for Architecture & Design brands. We bring deep industry fluency — not generic AI consulting.

What we do

Where sector knowledge meets design intelligence.

We understand the material culture of Architecture & Design — the pace of its cycles, the language of its objects, and the relationships that move it. That knowledge is the foundation of everything we build.

Our work spans brand strategy, creative direction, digital transformation, and AI integration. We do not apply generic frameworks. We build from the inside of the industry out.

MIAD was founded by two practitioners who have spent their careers in this space — one as a creative director shaping how design brands communicate, the other as a marketing leader inside the industry's most respected manufacturers.
